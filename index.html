How it works

From ions to insight

01

A real reaction engine

Solubility rules, the reactivity series, acid–base logic and redox rules decide what happens, so any combination works, including "no reaction" and the reason why.

02

Thermodynamics, not guesses

Enthalpy changes come from Hess's law using standard enthalpies of formation. E° and Ksp values show up in the deeper explanation.

03

See the invisible

Particle view shows ions colliding, solids forming and spectator ions standing by. Balanced molecular, ionic and net ionic equations are generated live.

0 chemicals ready

Lab Visuals is a teaching model, not a substitute for a real laboratory. Temperature changes assume 0.05 mol in 100 mL, so they are illustrative. Never repeat hazardous experiments (alkali metals, thermite, concentrated acids, lead and chromium salts) without a teacher, a fume cupboard and eye protection.
