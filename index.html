Lab Visuals is a teaching model, not a substitute for a real lab. Enthalpies come from standard thermodynamic data (ΔfH° at 298 K, Hess's law); temperature changes assume 0.05 mol in 100 mL of water, so they are illustrative. Never repeat dangerous experiments (alkali metals, thermite, conc. acids, lead and chromium salts) without a teacher, a fume cupboard and eye protection.
