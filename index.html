Built from real practical courses

Practice what your lab asks of you

The experiment list was built from real undergraduate and school practical syllabi, so what you rehearse here is what your lab course actually contains.

01

Measure like a chemist

Read the burette, spot the end point, record trials, calculate the strength, then see how close you were. Simulated readings carry random error, as in a real lab.

02

Find orders and energies

Run the H₂O₂ + KI clock at different concentrations and temperatures, then fit the order and the activation energy from your own data.

03

See the invisible

Switch to the particle view to watch ions collide, solids form and spectator ions stand by. Balanced equations are generated live with ΔH from Hess’s law.

Honest note: this is a rehearsal tool. It cannot teach hand skills such as pipetting, reading a real meniscus or filtering, and the numbers are textbook-approximate. Always follow your own lab manual and your teacher.
